How Does Choice Plumbing Perform Leak Detection and Pipe Repairs?

Choice Plumbing locates hidden leaks with electronic sensors and infrared cameras, then applies trenchless pipe lining or section replacements to fix damaged sections without extensive excavation.

Leak detection begins with moisture meters and acoustic amplifiers that trace water migration.

  • Infrared Scanning: Pinpoints temperature shifts behind walls and under slabs.
  • Pipe Lining: Cured-in-place pipe (CIPP) technology relines pipes to prevent future leaks.
  • Pipe Section Replacement: Cut-out repairs restore pressure integrity where lining isn’t feasible.

This approach reduces disruption, prevents structural damage, and restores plumbing integrity quickly.

Trenchless Pipe Lining

Trenchless pipe lining is a minimally invasive method for repairing damaged pipes, involving the insertion of an epoxy-soaked liner into the existing pipe to create a new, durable inner pipe. This technique avoids the need for extensive excavation, reducing disruption and costs.
